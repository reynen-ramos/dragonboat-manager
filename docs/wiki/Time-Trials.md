# Time Trials

Time trials answer what a crew sheet can't: **who is fast, and who is
getting faster**. A trial times individual paddlers — solo in an OC1, on the
erg, in a small boat — over a set distance, and the results feed selection
conversations and each paddler's progress view.

Find the module via the **Time trials** button on the Trainings page.

## Sessions

A session is one sitting: a **date**, a **distance** (200m, 500m — any
number), and a **discipline** (OC1 / Erg / Small boat by default;
[club-editable](Settings-and-Club-Rules#time-trial-disciplines)), with an
optional name like "Selection Trial I". **New session** creates one and
opens its sheet.

## Running a session

On the session page:

1. **Add paddlers** — search the active roster and add them one by one, or
   *Add all N* at once. People can be listed before they've run.
2. **Type times as they finish** — same entry style as
   [race day](Events-and-Race-Day#race-day): `1:05.42` or plain seconds,
   committed when you leave the box. Rows hold **name order** so nothing
   jumps under the stopwatch hand; the placement badge carries the ranking.
3. The ranking is live: gold badge for 1st, **ties share a placement**
   (then skip — two 1sts are followed by a 3rd), the gap to the fastest on
   every row, and untimed runs sit last, unplaced. The header counts
   "*n* of *m* timed". A note on a row ("Capsized at the turn — no time")
   explains an untimed listing.
4. **CSV** exports the ranked sheet; **Delete** removes the session and
   every time in it — with Undo.

## Best times

The Time trials page keeps a **Best times** board per *kind* of trial —
distance + discipline. A 200m OC1 time never shares a board with a 500m erg
score; comparisons only ever happen within one kind. Each board lists every
paddler's personal best, ranked with the same shared-placement rules, with
a bar showing the spread.

## Progress

Every member's page (and a paddler's own [My Page](My-Page-for-Paddlers))
gets a **Time trials** section once they have a timed run: their best per
kind, and every run drawn as a dated bar — **long is slow, short is fast,
so a shrinking stack reads as progress at a glance**. Dates link back to
their sessions.

## Time trials and crew selection

Deliberately, trial rankings do **not** feed the
[Fill boat](Lineups-and-the-Boat#fill-boat) ordering — who gets seated
first is club policy, and the app won't change that policy quietly. The
boards and progress views are there to inform the humans making the call.
