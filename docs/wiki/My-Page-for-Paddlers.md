# My Page — a paddler's guide

Signed in as a paddler, the app is your own corner of the club: your nav is
**My Page**, **Events**, and **Trainings** (with Sign out in the last slot
on a phone), and My Page is home.

> Seeing *"Your login isn't linked to the roster yet"*? Ask a coach to link
> your email to your member entry in Settings → Access. Everything below
> appears the moment they do.

## Are you in?

The top of My Page lists **every upcoming training and race** with three
buttons — **In / Maybe / Out**. Tap one; it saves instantly and your answer
lands on the coach's sheet in real time. A badge counts what you haven't
answered yet. Change your mind any time — tap a different answer.

This is the club asking, once, in one place. Answer honestly:

- **In** — plan the boat around me.
- **Maybe** — I might make it; the coach sees me as tentative everywhere.
- **Out** — don't wait for me (coaches appreciate an Out far more than
  silence).

## Your season

Below the sign-ups, your page shows the same truth the coach sees on your
member page:

- **Paddling** — your weight, side, preferred zones, roles, and season
  numbers: races and practices crewed, how often you said In when asked,
  and your *usual spot* in the boat.
- **Race results** — every race your crews recorded, ranked in its real
  field: your placement, "2nd of 6", the gap to the winner, the time.
- **Time trials** — your best per kind of trial and every run as a dated
  bar; shorter bars mean faster runs, so watch the stack shrink.
- **Coming up** and **History** — where you're seated (tap a badge to see
  the boat) and everything you've answered for.

## Your contact details

**Edit contact details** updates your email, phone, and emergency contact —
the one part of your roster entry you edit yourself. Keep the emergency
contact current; it's what the club holds for safety.

## What you can see elsewhere

**Events** and **Trainings** are fully readable — schedules, calendars,
lineups (yes, you can check who's seated where), race-day results as they're
typed, time-trial rankings, printable crew sheets. You'll find no edit
buttons there: seating, times, and the roster are the coaches' pens.
Clubmates' pages show paddling facts only — their contact details, weight,
and age are private, just as yours are from them.

On any event's sign-up sheet, your own row is live for you — same as
answering from My Page.
