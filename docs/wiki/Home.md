# Dragonboat Manager — User Guide

Dragonboat Manager runs a dragon boat club from a phone on the dock: the
roster, the training schedule, race entries, sign-ups, seating plans, time
trials, and the reports that make sense of a season.

It works two ways:

- **On one device, with no account** — everything is stored in your browser.
  Open the app and start. This is the default, and it is fully featured.
- **As a club, with sign-in** — a shared database, real accounts with
  admin / coach / paddler roles, live sync between devices, and paddlers
  answering their own sign-ups. See [Accounts and Roles](Accounts-and-Roles).

## The five-minute tour

1. Open the app, go to **Settings → Load demo club**. You get an 80-member
   roster, a season of trainings since New Year, a finished regatta with
   results, a race running *today* with heats ready to advance, and a
   part-planned championship.
2. **Dashboard** shows what's coming: races, trainings, and everything else,
   separately.
3. Open today's race → **Race day** — type a finish time, watch placements
   reorder themselves.
4. Open the championship → a crew → drag paddlers into seats, then try
   **Fill boat** and watch it seat everyone who signed up.
5. **Reports** — the attendance sheet a secretary would have built by hand.

Everything in the demo is disposable; **Settings → Clear everything** (or
loading the demo again) resets it.
