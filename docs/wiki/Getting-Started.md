# Getting Started

## Opening the app

Dragonboat Manager is a web app. Open it in any modern browser — phone,
tablet, or desktop. Nothing to install, no account needed to begin.

**Install it like an app (recommended on a phone):** in your browser's menu
choose *Add to Home Screen* (iOS Safari) or *Install app* (Android Chrome,
desktop Chrome/Edge). You get an icon, full-screen use, and the app opens
even without a connection (see [Backups and Your Data](Backups-and-Your-Data)
for what offline can and can't do).

The layout is built for one hand on a dock: on phones, navigation is a bar
at the **bottom** of the screen; on larger screens it is a sidebar.

## Where your data lives

Out of the box, everything is stored **in your browser on this device** —
private, instant, and working with no server at all. Two consequences:

- Clearing your browser's site data deletes your club. **Export a backup**
  regularly (Settings → Export backup); the app reminds you.
- Nothing syncs to other devices in this mode.

When the club is ready for shared, multi-device use with logins, see
[Accounts and Roles](Accounts-and-Roles) — the same app, backed by a real
database.

## Try the demo first

**Settings → Load demo club** fills the app with a large, realistic club:

- 80 members across every age division, with the messy edges of a real
  roster — missing weights, unknown birth dates, an inactive member, alumni.
- A full training season since New Year — water sessions every Saturday and
  Sunday, land training Tuesdays and Thursdays — with sign-ups answered and
  training boats seated, so attendance reports have something to say.
- A finished regatta with recorded times, a race running **today** with a
  timed heat waiting to be advanced, a part-planned championship (with an
  alternative "Plan B" lineup to compare), and a distant qualifier.
- Three time-trial sessions, including a shared first place and one
  capsized-no-time run.

The demo is anchored to *today* — load it any day and the season always
looks current. Load it again at any time to reset it.

## Setting up your own club

1. **Settings first.** Check [club rules](Settings-and-Club-Rules): the
   minimum number of women in a mixed crew (varies by governing body),
   balance tolerances, and the event types / training kinds / time-trial
   disciplines your club uses. Defaults are sensible; everything is
   editable.
2. **Roster next.** Add members by hand or [import a CSV](Members-and-Roster#importing-a-csv)
   of your existing spreadsheet.
3. **Schedule.** Create the training week in one dialog with a
   [repeating series](Trainings#repeating-trainings), and add your first
   race under [Events](Events-and-Race-Day).
4. **Back up.** Export a backup once real data exists, and after anything
   you'd hate to retype.

## A note on words the app uses

- **Sign-up** — a member saying *In*, *Maybe*, or *Out* for an event.
  The app deliberately says "answered" and "seated", never "attended":
  it records intentions and lineups, not a headcount at the dock.
- **Crew** — one boat's worth of people for one category of one event.
- **Category** — a class you enter at a race: boat size (10s or 20s),
  gender class (Open / Mixed / Women's), optionally an age division and
  distance.
- **Reserve** — travelling with a crew but not in a seat.
- **Plan (variant)** — an alternative lineup for a crew. Plans never race
  and never count in reports; they exist to be compared and, if better,
  swapped in.
