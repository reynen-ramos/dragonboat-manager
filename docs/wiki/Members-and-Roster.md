# Members and the Roster

The **Members** page is the club's roster. Each row shows the name, weight,
a gender mark (M / F / O), and a side mark — **L** (left only), **R** (right
only), or **B** (either side). Search and filters sit above the list; sort
by name, weight, or side.

## Adding a member

**Add member** opens the form:

| Field | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Name, gender | Gender drives Women's / Mixed crew rules and the composition report. |
| Date of birth | Derives age for age-division checks (Junior, U24, Premier, Senior A/B/C) and the reports' age bands. Optional — the app shows "unknown" honestly. |
| Weight | Feeds the boat's balance bars and the trim maths. Optional, but a boat balances better when most weights are recorded. |
| Side preference | Left only / Right only / Either side. The seating planner honours it, and the checks panel flags violations. |
| Preferred zones | Where along the boat they like to sit — **Stroke** (front pacers), **Engine Room** (middle), **Rockets** (back). Advisory: auto-balance tries to honour them, nothing enforces them. |
| Can drum / Can steer | Marks who can fill the drummer and coxswain roles — Fill boat uses this. |
| Status | **Active** (default — appears on sign-up sheets and in Fill boat), **Inactive** (kept, but out of daily flows), **Alumni** (history preserved, no longer paddling). |
| Contact, emergency contact, notes, joined date | The club records a safety sheet would hold. Notes are staff-only when logins exist. |

**Prefer status changes to deletion.** Deleting a member removes them from
every crew they were in. If someone leaves, set them to *Alumni* — their
race history stays intact. Deletion does offer **Undo** (a toast appears;
it restores everything the delete removed).

## The member page

Open any member for their whole story:

- **Paddling** — weight, side, zones, other roles, joined date, plus
  season numbers: races and practices crewed, "Said In" (answers given of
  times asked), and their *usual spot* (the seat zone and side they most
  often occupy).
- **Contact** — including the emergency contact.
- **Time trials** — their best time per kind of trial, with every timed run
  drawn as a bar: long is slow, short is fast, so a shrinking stack reads
  as progress. Each date links to the session.
- **Race results** — every race their crews recorded, ranked against the
  real field: placement, "2nd of 6", the gap to the winner, and the time.
- **Coming up** and **History** — every event they're seated at or answered
  for, newest first, with badges linking straight to the lineup.

## Importing a CSV

**Import CSV** accepts a spreadsheet export of your existing roster. The
dialog shows exactly which columns it understood and previews the rows
before anything is written; rows with problems are listed with reasons, and
you choose to proceed with the good ones or fix the file. A CSV export of
the current roster (from the same page) doubles as a template.

## Filters worth knowing

- **Status filter** defaults to Active — the roster you actually paddle
  with. Switch it to see Inactive or Alumni members.
- **Gender and side filters** mirror the sign-up sheet's, so "who are my
  available left-side women" is answerable in two taps on either page.
